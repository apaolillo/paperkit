\section{Approach}

The bibliography check reads the citations from the build output, not from
the sources, so a commented-out \verb|\cite| or a key built with a macro does
not fool it. Each entry nothing cites is reported with its key, and the
report says which target it looked at. Table~\ref{tab:entries} lists the
entries of this paper.

\begin{table}[t]
  \caption{The bibliography entries of this paper and their state.}
  \label{tab:entries}
  \centering
  \begin{tabular}{lll}
    \toprule
    Key & Cited & Reported \\
    \midrule
    \texttt{llncs} & yes & no \\
    \texttt{reproducible} & yes & no \\
    \texttt{extended} & no & no, excused by key \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}
