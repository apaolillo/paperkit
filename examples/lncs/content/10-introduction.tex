\section{Introduction}

Springer compiles the sources of an LNCS paper itself, and its checklist asks
for the compiled bibliography, the \texttt{.bbl} file, next to the
main file~\cite{llncs}. A page count in LNCS proceedings includes the
references, so the budget is a cap on the whole PDF. paperkit handles both
from the configuration file: the archive carries the \texttt{.bbl}, and the
report flags the PDF when it grows past the cap~\cite{reproducible}.

This example also shows a bibliography with one entry more than the paper
cites. The entry is kept for the extended version of the paper and is excused
by key, so the unused-entry check stays on for everything else.
