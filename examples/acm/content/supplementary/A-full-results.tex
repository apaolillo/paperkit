\section{Full Results}

Table~\ref{tab:full} lists every check with the state it can be in. The paper
only shows the result for this project; a check is also \emph{skipped} when
the tool it needs is missing, which paperkit reports rather than hides, since
a skipped check is not a passed one.

\begin{table}[h]
  \caption{The states a check can end in.}
  \label{tab:full}
  \begin{tabular}{ll}
    \toprule
    State & Meaning \\
    \midrule
    clean & ran, nothing to report \\
    warning & ran, something to look at, the build is usable \\
    error & ran, the PDF should not be submitted \\
    skipped & did not run, a tool or an input is missing \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}
